Important Information

Policies & Payments

Lead time

Most orders need 3–5 days notice. Party packages need at least 1 week.

Deposit

A 50% non-refundable deposit is required after we send your quote to confirm the order.

Payments

After we review your request, we'll send your final price and a secure Stripe payment link for the 50% non-refundable deposit to confirm your order.

The remaining balance is due the day before pickup or delivery. We'll reach out via text or Instagram within 24 hours.

Cancellations

Deposits are non-refundable. Please give 48 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule.

Allergies & Delivery

Please list any food allergies before ordering. Delivery is available in the Miami area for a small additional fee; pickup is also available.

Ordering Timeline

Most orders need 3–5 days notice. Party packages need at least 1 week. No same-day orders.

Liability

Once your order is picked up or dropped off, all responsibility for its care transfers to you. We're not liable for any issues after that.
