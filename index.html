Our Story & Process

Daily sweetness, made with care

Satisfy your cravings with handcrafted desserts — flan, tres leches, cakes & more. Every order is made with quality ingredients, whether you're celebrating or just treating yourself.

Ordering is simple: choose treats, request with notice, confirm with a deposit, then pick up or get delivery.

1. Choose your treats

Browse the menu and add items. Customize flavors, sizes, and decorations.

2. Request your date

Submit your request with the date you need and any special details.

3. Review & secure payment

We review your request, send your final price, then a secure Stripe link to confirm.

4. Pickup or Delivery

Fresh on your chosen date. Delivery available in the Miami area for a small additional fee.

Lead time: Most orders need 3–5 days notice. Party packages need at least 1 week.

Deposit: A 50% non-refundable deposit is required after we send your quote to confirm the order.

Allergies: Please list any dietary needs in the order form.
